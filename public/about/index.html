About Tripp Coleman

Tripp Coleman, known on stage as Trippmagic, is a self-taught magician from Pilot Butte, Saskatchewan, who has been performing since 2017. He has worked the streets of Las Vegas and performed for Capital GMC, John Deere, eXp Realty and the Conexus Arts Centre.

Background

Tripp started with simple card tricks as a kid and taught himself the rest. He has been performing professionally since 2017, building his skills one audience at a time instead of through a magic school.

He has performed street magic in Las Vegas several times. Working a sidewalk crowd that never planned to stop is one of the toughest tests in magic, and it shaped the fast, interactive style he brings to every show.

Style

Tripp's magic is close-up, interactive and funny. He blends sleight of hand, card magic, street magic and comedy, with illusions for bigger stage shows. Audience members don't just watch: they hold the cards, call the choices and are often the ones the magic happens to.
