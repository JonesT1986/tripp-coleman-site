Planning tips for Regina events

Regina events range from basement birthday parties to ballroom galas, and Tripp adjusts the format to fit. For a home party, a cleared living room is plenty. For a hotel or hall, let him know the room size so he can decide between strolling magic and a stage set with a headset microphone and PA.

If your event is downtown, tell Tripp about parking or loading so he arrives ready to start on time.

Travel and scheduling in Regina

Because Pilot Butte sits right on Regina's east edge, Tripp can usually reach any Regina neighbourhood in well under half an hour. If your date is fixed, reach out early: weekend evenings are usually the first slots to go.
