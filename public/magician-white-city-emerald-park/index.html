Business events in Emerald Park

Emerald Park's business community makes it a natural fit for corporate magic: staff appreciation nights, customer events and holiday parties, with strolling close-up magic that needs no stage.

Planning tips for White City and Emerald Park

Backyard parties are common here, so plan a spot out of strong wind for the magic: a patio, garage or shaded corner works. For indoor parties, a cleared basement or living room is plenty. Business events in Emerald Park can use strolling magic at a staff party or customer event with no stage or sound needed.
