Magician in Balgonie, Saskatchewan

Tripp Coleman performs magic in Balgonie, a short drive east of his home base in Pilot Butte along Highway 1. He's a fit for birthday parties, school and library programs, and community events in town.

Small-town events, big reactions

In a community like Balgonie, the whole town shows up to the event. Tripp's strolling magic is built for that kind of crowd: he works through the room group by group, so the kids at the front and the grandparents at the back all get a turn.

Popular Balgonie bookings

  • Birthday parties
  • School and library shows
  • Community events, fundraisers and family reunions
  • Wedding receptions

Planning tips for Balgonie events

Community halls, rinks and school gyms are typical spaces for Balgonie events. In a big room, Tripp uses a wireless headset microphone and PA for a stage set, or he can stroll through the crowd group by group with no sound at all. Tell him which setup your venue has.

Travel

Balgonie is a quick trip down Highway 1 from Pilot Butte. Tripp also covers White City and Emerald Park and Regina, and travels up to about 200 miles for events.
