Why companies book a magician

Magic breaks the ice faster than anything on the agenda. At a mixer, people who don't know each other end up laughing at the same impossible moment, and then they're talking. At a client night, it gives your guests a story to take back to the office.

Tripp has performed for Capital GMC, John Deere, eXp Realty and the Conexus Arts Centre, along with cocktail parties and private events in Regina. He has also performed on the streets of Las Vegas several times, which is about the toughest training ground there is for winning over a crowd that didn't plan to stop.

Formats for corporate events

Strolling close-up magic

Tripp moves table to table or group to group during a reception, performing card magic right in guests' hands. No stage or sound is needed, and it suits cocktail hours, networking events and trade-show booths.

Stage show

A full show for a seated audience after dinner or as part of a program. For stage shows Tripp uses a wireless headset microphone and a PA system, so tell him about the room when you book.

Branded moments

Want your product, logo or company message to show up in the magic? Ask. A custom reveal is a memorable way to close a launch or award night.

What's included

  • Strolling, stage, or a mix of both, 60 to 120 minutes in total
  • Clean, crowd-appropriate material for professional audiences
  • A pre-event call to match the show to your guests and schedule
  • Travel within the Regina area, with farther events quoted on request

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the show appropriate for professional audiences?

Yes. Tripp tailors material to the crowd, and his corporate work includes events for Capital GMC, John Deere, eXp Realty and the Conexus Arts Centre.

Do you need a stage?

Not for strolling close-up magic. For a stage show, Tripp needs space at the front of the room plus a wireless headset microphone and PA system.

Can magic include our brand or product?

Yes. Ask about a branded reveal or a custom moment built around your company or launch.

How long does a corporate set run?

Tripp's sets run 60 to 120 minutes in total and can be split between a strolling portion and a stage portion.

Do you perform at trade shows?

Yes. Close-up magic at a booth draws a crowd and gives your team a reason to start conversations.
