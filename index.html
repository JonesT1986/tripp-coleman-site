Meet Tripp

Close enough to touch. Impossible to explain.

Tripp Coleman started with simple card tricks as a kid and taught himself the rest. Performing since 2017, he's worked the streets of Las Vegas several times over, plus corporate rooms and stages across Saskatchewan, and he's building a brand-new show.

He works inches from your face, with a deck and nowhere to hide. Cards rise on their own, vanish from your hand, and turn up where they have no business being. No velvet capes, no cheesy patter. Just sharp sleight of hand and the kind of moment people are still describing the next morning.
